## Idea 2: "Jungle Cruise Ride Queue Board"

**Design Movement:** Theme park signage and ride queue environmental design — weathered wooden signs, hand-painted lettering, tropical dockside aesthetic.

**Core Principles:**
1. Weathered wood plank textures as primary surface material
2. Hand-painted sign lettering with playful imperfections
3. Tropical foliage framing every section like overgrown jungle
4. Playful wayfinding mimicking a theme park queue experience

**Color Philosophy:** Warm wood brown (#6B4226) structural base, deep jungle green (#1B4332) foliage, sun-bleached cream (#FFF8E7) sign faces, faded red (#C0392B) price tags, river blue (#2980B9) interactive elements. Golden hour at a tropical dock.

**Layout Paradigm:** Vertical scroll like walking through a ride queue — each section is a "station" with wooden sign headers. Products on "bulletin board" pinned cards. Budget tiers as ticket classes (Economy Cruise, Deluxe Expedition, Captain's Charter).

**Signature Elements:** Wooden plank headers with carved lettering, pinned index cards with thumbtack shadows, rope-wrapped section dividers

**Interaction Philosophy:** Category filters as dock destination boards. Cart as "Cargo Manifest." Rope-pull toggle filters.

**Animation:** Wooden signs swing on load, cards pin with bounce, rope dividers tighten on scroll.

**Typography:** Permanent Marker headers, Bebas Neue subheaders/prices, Work Sans body text

## Idea 3: "Expedition Supply Co. — Tropical Outfitter"

**Design Movement:** Mid-century adventure brand catalog meets modern outdoor retailer — bold graphic shapes, retro-futuristic optimism.

**Core Principles:**
1. Bold geometric shapes and diagonal cuts creating dynamic energy
2. Flat color blocks with halftone dot textures for vintage print feel
3. Oversized typography commanding attention like travel posters
4. Items presented as essential expedition gear

**Color Philosophy:** Deep navy (#0A1628) background, sunset orange (#E8751A) primary accent, tropical teal (#1ABC9C) secondary, cream (#FDF6EC) content areas, rich gold (#D4A017) featured items. 1960s adventure brand confidence.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-width hero with diagonal clip-path transitions. Masonry product grid. Budget tiers as "expedition packages" with stamp badges. Sticky category bar as ship's instrument panel.

**Signature Elements:** Diagonal section transitions with halftone fades, circular badge/stamp motifs, retro binocular/compass iconography

**Typography:** Bebas Neue oversized headers, Space Grotesk subheaders, DM Sans body
