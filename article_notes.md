## Article 1: "Before You Reboot, Audit What You Already Built"

**Type**: Full LinkedIn Article
**Published**: October 21, 2025
**Read Time**: 7 minutes
**URL**: https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/before-you-reboot-audit-what-already-built-jeff-qsd3e

### Summary
Challenges the common impulse to "reboot" Agile transformations when things aren't working. Instead of starting over with new consultants and playbooks, Jeff argues teams should audit what they've already built.

### Key Themes
1. **The "Just This Once" Trap** - How discipline erodes gradually through small exceptions
2. **The Déjà Vu Moment** - Recognizing that solutions may already exist from previous efforts
3. **The Audit Approach** - Systematically reviewing existing artifacts
4. **Connecting Old Process to Current Pain** - Tying existing materials to current frustrations
